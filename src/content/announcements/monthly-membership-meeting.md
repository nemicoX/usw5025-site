The monthly membership meeting is where the local makes its decisions. This is
your chance to hear what's going on, ask questions, and vote on what matters.

**When:** Posted here and on the floor. Check back for the date and time.

**Where:** Union hall.

Bring your questions. The more members in the room, the stronger the local.
