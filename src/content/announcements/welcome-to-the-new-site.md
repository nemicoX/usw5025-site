This is the new website for USW Local 5025-02. We built it to give members one
reliable place to find union news, the contract, and the resources you need,
without digging through emails or the bulletin board.

## What you'll find here

- **News** — announcements, meeting times, and updates, posted as they happen.
- **Documents** — the current contract, the elections manual, and other reference material.
- **Contact** — a direct line to the local's officers for questions, grievances, or ideas.

## Bookmark it

Save this page on your phone. When something changes on the floor or a meeting
gets scheduled, this is where it goes first.

In solidarity,
The officers of Local 5025-02
